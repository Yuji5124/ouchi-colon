# Phase 3E.10「自分で遊びを発見」

- Colon offers a local, authored play-discovery invitation only after the child explicitly asks to find a play idea.
- The child chooses the play subject. Once a recognized subject is spoken, the existing imaginative-play engine continues the child's idea.
- The invitation can be declined or left unanswered without a follow-up prompt. Safety and privacy responses remain higher priority.
- Only the existing short-lived play context is used. Speech text is not added to persistent storage, and no camera, network service, or timer is used to discover a play idea.
